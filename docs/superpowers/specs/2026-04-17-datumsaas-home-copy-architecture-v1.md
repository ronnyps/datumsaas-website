# DatumSaaS Home Copy Architecture v1

## Objective
Create a full-page copy structure for the DatumSaaS Home page that is clear, enterprise-ready, and conversion-focused.

Primary action:
- Book a demo.

Audience:
- US companies scaling operations, finance, and team execution.
- Buyers: operations leaders, finance leaders, founders, and department heads.

Voice:
- Clear, competent, outcome-focused.
- Professional and direct.
- No hype language.

---

## Page Structure (AIDA + Conversion Flow)

1. `Navigation`
2. `Attention` Hero
3. `Trust Bar` (proof cue strip)
4. `Interest` Operating Model (bento narrative)
5. `Desire` Why DatumSaaS (dark confidence block)
6. `How It Works` (3-step adoption path)
7. `Objection Handling` (FAQ)
8. `Action` Final CTA
9. `Footer`
