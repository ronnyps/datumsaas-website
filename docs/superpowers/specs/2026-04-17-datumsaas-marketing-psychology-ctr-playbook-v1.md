# DatumSaas Marketing Psychology CTR Playbook v1

Date: 2026-04-17
Scope: CTA copy and intent optimization for homepage buttons (EN/ES) using ethical marketing psychology.

## 1) Objective

Increase click-through rate on homepage CTAs by improving:
- action clarity,
- perceived relevance,
- risk reduction,
- decision confidence.

## 2) Psychological Models Applied

1. Specificity Effect
- Why: specific CTAs convert better than generic ones.
- Applied as: "45-min demo" and "go-live plan".

2. Present Bias
- Why: people prefer immediate, concrete value.
- Applied as: "See your workflow live" vs generic "Learn more".

3. Regret Aversion
- Why: buyers avoid decisions that might backfire.
- Applied as: "No hard commitment" + phased adoption language.

4. Status-Quo Bias Reduction
- Why: switching feels risky.
- Applied as: "Start with one workflow, not all-at-once migration."

5. Goal Framing
- Why: clear outcome framing drives action.
- Applied as: "Get my go-live plan" + "clear 90-day improvement targets."

## 3) CTA Changes Implemented

## EN
- Hero primary CTA:
  - From: `Request a Demo`
  - To: `Request Your 45-Min Demo`
- Hero secondary CTA:
  - From: `See How It Works`
  - To: `See Your Workflow Live`
- Demo process CTA:
  - From: `Request a Demo`
  - To: `Get My Go-Live Plan`
- Final CTA button:
  - From: `Request a Demo`
  - To: `Request My 45-Min Demo`
- Final CTA micro-trust:
  - To: `No hard commitment. Start with one workflow, not a forced all-at-once migration.`

## ES
- Hero CTA principal:
  - De: `Solicitar demo`
  - A: `Solicitar demo de 45 min`
- Hero CTA secundario:
  - De: `Ver cómo funciona`
  - A: `Ver tu flujo en vivo`
- CTA de proceso de demo:
  - De: `Solicitar demo`
  - A: `Recibir plan de go-live`
- CTA final:
  - De: `Solicitar demo`
  - A: `Solicitar mi demo de 45 min`
- Micro-trust final:
  - A: `Sin compromiso forzado. Empieza por un flujo, sin migración total de golpe.`

## 4) Why This Should Improve CTR

- Lower ambiguity: users know what they are requesting.
- Higher relevance: "your workflow" personalizes intent.
- Lower anxiety: migration fear is addressed before click.
- Better motivation: immediate, structured outcome is explicit.

## 5) A/B Test Plan (Recommended)

Test 1: Hero primary CTA
- Variant A: `Request Your 45-Min Demo`
- Variant B: `Get My Go-Live Plan`
- Metric: Hero CTA CTR + form completion rate

Test 2: Final CTA micro-trust
- Variant A: migration reassurance
- Variant B: no-obligation reassurance
- Metric: Final CTA CTR + demo qualified rate

Test 3: Secondary CTA
- Variant A: `See Your Workflow Live`
- Variant B: `See How It Works`
- Metric: Secondary CTA CTR + downstream primary CTA clicks

## 6) Guardrails (Brand + Ethics)

- No fake scarcity.
- No fabricated proof.
- No inflated promises.
- Maintain clear, competent, outcome-focused tone.
- Keep EN/ES semantic parity for Miami bilingual audience.

## 7) Next Step

After 2-3 weeks of traffic:
- keep winner variants by CTA location,
- then optimize only one element at a time (label, supporting line, or placement) to avoid noisy data.
