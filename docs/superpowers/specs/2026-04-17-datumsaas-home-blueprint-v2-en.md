# DatumSaaS Home Blueprint v2 (EN)

## Version Goal
Condense the homepage into a stronger authority narrative with less duplication, clearer proof flow, and one dominant conversion action.

Primary objective:
- Position DatumSaaS as an all-in-one real operating system for mid-market companies across sectors.

Primary CTA:
- `Book a Strategic Demo`

---

## Core Strategic Direction

- Audience model: committee buying (`Operations`, `Finance`, `Leadership`).
- Core pain:
  - Disconnected tools
  - Loss of control while scaling
- Message spine:
  - `One system replaces fragmented workflows and restores control at scale.`

---

## Homepage Structure v2 (Reduced and Non-Redundant)

1. Header
2. Hero
3. Fragmentation Cost
4. End-to-End System Flow
5. Capability Bento (4 core cards)
6. Hierarchy and Governance (differentiator)
7. Role-Based Value (tabs)
8. Demo Process (3 steps)
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA
11. Footer

---

## Section Blueprint

## 1) Header
Purpose:
- Orientation and trust.

Copy:
- Nav: `Platform`, `How It Works`, `Why DatumSaaS`, `Security`, `Documentation`
- CTA: `Book a Strategic Demo`

Proof behavior:
- Sticky glass header.
- Subtle status chip: `Built for growing teams`.

## 2) Hero
Purpose:
- Deliver clear value in one sentence.

Copy:
- H1: `One System to Run Your Entire Business`
- Sub: `Manage leads, work, sales, payments, and reporting in one place, so teams stay aligned as you grow.`
- CTA 1: `Book a Strategic Demo`
- CTA 2: `See How It Works`

Proof behavior:
- Animated flow strip: `Lead -> Task -> Sale -> Payment -> Report`.
- One before/after visual:
  - Before: tool fragmentation
  - After: connected operating flow

## 3) Fragmentation Cost
Purpose:
- Make pain concrete and universal.

Copy:
- Title: `When Tools Are Disconnected, Growth Slows Down`
- Body: `Teams work in separate systems, data conflicts, and decisions take longer. Scaling adds complexity instead of control.`
- Points:
  - `Missed handoffs`
  - `Repeated manual work`
  - `Limited cross-team visibility`

Proof behavior:
- Split panel (`broken stack` vs `connected system map`).

## 4) End-to-End System Flow
Purpose:
- Show single continuous operational journey.

Copy:
- Title: `From First Contact to Final Report, In One Flow`
- Steps:
  1. Capture and qualify
  2. Assign and execute
  3. Create orders and proposals
  4. Manage payments
  5. Review performance

Proof behavior:
- Interactive horizontal timeline.
- Step reveal with real UI snapshots.

## 5) Capability Bento (4 Core Cards)
Purpose:
- Demonstrate depth without overload.

Cards:
1. `Lead to Customer Management`
2. `Operations and Team Scheduling`
3. `Sales, Orders, and Payments`
4. `Reporting and Automation`

Rule:
- 4 cards only on Home.
- Full feature depth moves to future feature pages.

Proof behavior:
- Dense bento with one key outcome label per card.

## 6) Hierarchy and Governance (Differentiator)
Purpose:
- Show why DatumSaaS is stronger than basic tool bundles.

Copy:
- Title: `Control Multiple Units from One Structure`
- Body: `Manage clients, branches, or dependencies through a parent structure while each team keeps role-based access.`

Proof behavior:
- Animated tree map:
  - Parent account -> child entities -> teams

## 7) Role-Based Value
Purpose:
- Align committee stakeholders quickly.

Copy:
- Operations: `Clearer ownership and faster execution.`
- Finance: `Stronger payment and performance control.`
- Leadership: `One reliable view for faster decisions.`

Proof behavior:
- 3 tabs with screenshot + KPI context per role.

## 8) Demo Process
Purpose:
- Lower friction to book meeting.

Copy:
- Title: `What Happens in a Strategic Demo`
- Steps:
  1. Understand your current setup
  2. Show your workflow in DatumSaaS
  3. Define fit and rollout priorities
- CTA: `Book a Strategic Demo`

Proof behavior:
- Vertical 3-step timeline with expected durations.

## 9) FAQ
Purpose:
- Resolve adoption objections.

Questions:
- Replace all tools at once?
- Suitable across sectors?
- Too complex for teams?
- Can leadership and teams align in one system?

Proof behavior:
- Clean accordion, short answers.

## 10) Final CTA
Purpose:
- Close with confidence and one action.

Copy:
- H2: `See Your Real Operation Running in One System`
- Body: `Book a strategic session and review your workflow with our team.`
- CTA: `Book a Strategic Demo`
- Secondary: `Talk to Sales`

Proof behavior:
- High-contrast block with subtle motion background.

## 11) Footer
Purpose:
- Authority and trust routes.

Copy:
- Value line: `DatumSaaS helps growing companies run operations, finance, and execution with clarity and control.`

## Motion and Microinteraction Map (v2)

- Hero:
  - Staggered reveal (title -> subtitle -> CTA -> flow strip).
- System Flow:
  - Scroll-linked step activation.
- Bento:
  - Soft hover lift + scale.
- Hierarchy block:
  - Tree expansion animation.
- Role tabs:
  - Crossfade content transition.
- CTA:
  - Subtle active press interaction.

Motion guardrails:
- No decorative overload.
- Transform/opacity only.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
